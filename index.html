Visual Storytelling That Brings Your Brand to Life

I'm Guy Bracha, a graphic designer and visual storyteller based in Herzliya, Israel, specializing in character design, brand identity, and creative marketing materials.

What I Offer:
✦ Character Design & Illustration – Bring your characters and stories to life with unique, memorable designs
✦ Brand Identity & Logo Design – Create cohesive visual identities that make your brand stand out
✦ Marketing & Advertising Materials – Design eye-catching visuals for print, digital, and social media
✦ Comic Art & Sequential Storytelling – Develop engaging visual narratives with manga-inspired layouts
✦ Pop Art & Illustration – Bold, vibrant designs that make an impact

My Approach:
I combine creative vision with technical precision, using Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign to deliver professional results. Drawing inspiration from filmmakers like David Lynch and Terry Gilliam, as well as anime aesthetics from Naruto and JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, I create designs that tell compelling stories.

I also leverage cutting-edge AI tools like Midjourney and ChatGPT to explore innovative concepts and accelerate the creative process, ensuring every project is both unique and efficiently delivered.

Whether you need a complete brand identity, character designs for your project, or marketing materials that convert, I'm here to transform your vision into stunning visual reality.

Let's create something extraordinary together.

Professional Experience

  • 2025: Graphic Designer at Karina Printed Shirts – Creating marketing materials, website layouts, and brand identity designs
  • 2017-2019: Civil Service (She'rot Le'umi) at Access Israel – Supporting accessibility initiatives

Creative Skills & Tools

  • Design Software: Adobe Illustrator (Vector Art), Adobe Photoshop (Image Editing), Adobe InDesign (Layout Design)
  • Character Design: Original superhero characters, costume design, visual identity systems
  • Comic Art: Panel layouts, sequential storytelling, manga-inspired aesthetics
  • World Building: Universe creation, power systems, narrative structures
  • Branding & Marketing: Logo design, advertising materials, website layouts
  • AI Tools: Midjourney (Visual Generation), ChatGPT (Creative Development), GitHub Copilot
  • Visual Influences: David Lynch, Terry Gilliam, Brian De Palma, Naruto, Fullmetal Alchemist, JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

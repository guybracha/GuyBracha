Bridging the Gap Between Creative Design & Technical Execution

I'm Guy Bracha, a marketing-oriented Graphic Designer & Illustrator with a unique background in Software Engineering. Based in Herzliya, Israel, I specialize in transforming conceptual ideas into high-impact visual assets, monetization banners, and engaging digital layouts.

What I Bring to the Table:
✦ Monetization & Live Ops Design – Creating high-converting promotional banners, icons, and pop-ups tailored for casual gaming aesthetics.
✦ Commercial Branding & Merchandising – Developing cohesive brand identities and production-ready marketing assets (as demonstrated in my work for Karina Printed Shirts).
✦ Technical Advantage – My engineering background allows me to understand development workflows, structure clean project files (Photoshop/Illustrator), and collaborate seamlessly with developers and technical teams.

Tools & Ecosystem:
Expertise in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign, combined with an analytical mindset to tackle complex layout challenges.
